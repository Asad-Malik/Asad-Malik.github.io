Assistant Research Professor · NSPARC, Mississippi State University

Asad Waqar Malik

I build next-generation AI systems that connect foundation models to real-world cyber-physical environments. My work centers on agentic AI, large language models, and multi-agent orchestration: autonomous agents that reason, collaborate and carry out complex workflows across edge, cloud and IoT infrastructure under tight limits on latency, reliability, security and compute.

I am Associate Editor of IEEE Access and Co-PI on a $1M NSF-funded AI project. My work appears in IEEE Internet Computing, IEEE Internet of Things Journal, IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics, Scientific Reports and other venues.
